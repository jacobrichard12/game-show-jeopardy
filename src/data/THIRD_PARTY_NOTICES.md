# Third-party content

## game-words

Most of the `act` (Act / Draw / Describe) and `charades` (Reverse Charades)
words in `partyGames.json` are a curated selection from
[nick-aschenbach/game-words](https://github.com/nick-aschenbach/game-words)
(commit `5301da97e9fc46813e4a60d6d348a1d40eda6a2b`), imported with
`scripts/game-words/import.js`. The hand-written entries in those games are
this project's own.
